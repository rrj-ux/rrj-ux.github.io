Little Stories Privacy Policy

Little Stories is an offline illustrated story app for children, made by Ralph Joiner. This policy applies to Little Stories and explains what happens when your family uses the app or contacts us.

Listening and saved progress

Stories, narration, and pictures come with the app. Listening does not require an account or an internet connection. The app saves your place in unfinished stories using the device’s app settings. Little Stories does not send that progress to us. You can clear saved progress in For Parents. Apple’s device backup settings may apply to app settings.

Children’s privacy

The story player does not ask children for names, ages, contact details, or other personal information. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, or location to play stories. Little Stories does not include ads, social features, or purchases within the story player.

Contacting us

A parent or guardian can email ralphjoinerjr@gmail.com for support or a privacy question. We receive the email address and information you include and use it to respond. Please do not send a child’s personal information. If you believe a child’s information reached us, contact us so we can remove it.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store downloads and TestFlight distribution under its own privacy policies. Your device settings control optional diagnostics or beta feedback that you share through Apple.

Updates

If the app changes how it handles information, we’ll revise this policy before making the change available.
