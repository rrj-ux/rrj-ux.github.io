Safety Guide

Yardago gets children running, freezing and dodging. Please read this before the first game and keep it in mind every time.

A grown-up is always in charge

  • Watch the players for the whole game. The app runs the game. It does not watch over the children, and it cannot see danger.
  • You can stop at any time with STOP on the screen, or simply by calling everyone in. Your judgement always comes before the app's.
  • The app can get things wrong. It may miss a player, mix two players up or make a wrong call. Treat its calls as part of the game, not as a referee you must obey.

Choose and check the play area

  • Flat, open ground with a soft, even surface such as grass. Avoid gravel, wet or icy ground, mud, and slopes steep enough to make running unsteady.
  • Keep well away from pools, ponds and other water; roads, driveways and parked cars; steps, walls, drops and ditches; trampolines, play equipment, furniture, trees, fences and anything with sharp edges.
  • Walk the whole area first and remove toys, tools, stones, sticks, hoses, dog waste and anything else that could trip someone.
  • Mark anything you cannot move, such as a tree or a bench, as an obstacle during setup, and still tell the players about it. The app keeps zones away from marked obstacles but players can still run into them.
  • Only confirm an area as safe if it really is. The app may send players to any part of it.
  • Leave space around the edges. Players often run past a finish line or out of a zone.

Place the phone safely

  • Put the phone on a stable stand or tripod on firm ground, or lean it securely against something steady, where it can't be knocked over.
  • Put it outside the play area, where nobody will run into it and where it cannot fall on anyone.
  • Keep the phone out of direct sun on hot days. It can overheat, and the app may slow down or stop.

Before you play

  • Proper shoes for running are best. Avoid playing barefoot on unknown ground.
  • Check the weather. Do not play in lightning, strong wind, ice or extreme heat or cold. Bring everyone in if the weather turns.
  • Play in good light. The app needs to see the players, and players need to see the ground.
  • Water and shade on warm days. Take breaks, and watch for signs that someone is too hot or tired.
  • Health first. Check with a doctor before strenuous play for anyone with a heart, breathing, joint or other medical condition, or who is recovering from an injury.
  • Explain the rules and agree a way to stop, for example "when the grown-up shouts STOP, everyone freezes".

During each game

  • Lava Field and Invisible Maze: players watch the ground, not the phone. Remind them to walk or jog carefully and to look where they are going.
  • Red Light, Green Light: freezing suddenly can make players stumble. Make sure there is space between players, and no pushing.
  • Treasure Hunt: several players may run towards the same spot. Remind them to look out for each other.
  • Simon Says: only do moves each player can do safely. Skip anything that does not suit the space or the players.
  • Keep numbers small enough for the space: up to four players, with plenty of room each.
  • Stop play straight away if anyone is hurt, upset, unwell, or if someone who is not playing, a pet or a vehicle comes into the area.

Batteries and the phone

The app keeps the camera on and the screen awake, so the phone uses its battery quickly and may get warm. Let it cool down if it becomes hot, and do not leave a charging phone where it could be stepped on or get wet.
