Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

This policy explains how the Yardago app ("the app") handles information. The app is made by Ralph Joiner ("we", "us").

The short version

  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information. We have no servers that the app talks to.
  • The camera picture never leaves the phone. The app looks at it on the phone, in memory, to find where players are standing, and then discards it.
  • The app does not use the internet. It contains no code that connects to the internet (on Android it does not even ask for internet access), so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Everything the app saves stays on your phone, and you can delete it at any time.

Who uses the app

The app is meant to be set up and run by an adult: a parent, guardian, teacher or other supervising grown-up. Children take part in the games by moving around the play area. They do not need to touch the phone, and the app does not ask anyone for a name, age, email address or any other contact details.

What the app uses on your phone

Camera. The app needs the camera to see the play area and the players. Each camera picture is analysed on the phone by a built-in person-detection model, which works out where each person is standing. The app also notes the colour of each player's top so it can tell players apart and call them by colour (for example "Red" or "Blue"). Pictures are not saved, and shirt colours are kept in memory only for the current game.

Speaker. The app speaks its instructions using the phone's own text-to-speech engine and plays sound effects. The app passes the words to be spoken to that engine on the phone. If you have set up a text-to-speech engine that uses the internet, its handling is governed by its provider's privacy policy.

Vibration. The app may vibrate the phone for alerts.

The app does not use the microphone, location, contacts, photos, files outside its own folder, or any account on the phone.

What the app saves on your phone

The app saves the following in its own private folder on the phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Settings, such as distance units, the start countdown and the sound settings.
  • Saved yards: the measured size and shape of your play area, marked obstacles and the camera's position. If you set up the yard by walking its corners, the height you typed in is saved so the app can measure the yard next time.
  • Saved courses for Lava Field.
  • Game records: a text record of each game (for example which zone a player entered, finishing times and scores). Players are identified only by a number or shirt colour. Game records contain no pictures and no names. Game records are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Testing recorder (under Settings, Developer tools, and off unless you switch it on): when on, it saves a detailed text log, still pictures from the camera, pictures of the app's screens, and recordings of the app's own voice, so that problems can be investigated. These files stay on the phone in the app's folder. They are never sent anywhere by the app. You can delete them with "Delete all saved data", and you should not share them unless you are comfortable sharing the pictures they contain.

The app's saved files (yards, courses, game records and testing files) are excluded from backups: on Android the app's folder is not copied to cloud backups, and on iPhone they are not copied to iCloud or computer backups. On iPhone, your settings (such as game and sound choices, and that you accepted these terms) may be included in your phone's own backup.

Children's privacy

We care about children's privacy, and the app is designed so that no personal information about children is collected by us at all. In particular:

  • We do not collect personal information from children, as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or similar laws, because no information leaves the phone.
  • Children are not asked for any information, and there is no chat, sharing, social feature, link to websites, or in-app purchase.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking code.

If you believe that information about a child has somehow reached us, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your choices and your rights

  • Delete everything: Settings, Legal & safety, Delete all saved data. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it saved.
  • Camera access: you can withdraw camera permission in your phone's settings at any time. The games cannot run without it.

Because we do not hold any personal information about you or your children, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. If you live in a place with data-protection rights, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you can still contact us with any question or request and we will answer it.

Security

Information stays on your phone, protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock. The app stores it in its private folder, which other apps cannot read. On iPhone you can see the app's folder in the Files app, so that you can send testing files if you choose to.

App stores

When you download the app, Google Play or the Apple App Store may collect information about you under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal information about you from them beyond the anonymous totals they show all developers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and show the new policy in the app before you continue using it.
